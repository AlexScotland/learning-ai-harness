## 🥩 The Steak Rub (Smoky, Sweet & Savory)
Mix these ingredients together in a small bowl. This ratio makes enough for 2–4 steaks: [2, 3] 

* 
* 1 tbsp Brown sugar (creates a beautiful, caramelized crust)
* 1 tbsp Chili powder (adds deep, earthy flavor)
* 1 tbsp Paprika (for rich color and mild sweetness)
* 1 tsp Salt
* 1 tsp Black pepper
* 1/2 tsp Italiano seasoning (adds a nice herbal undertone that complements beef)
* A pinch Red pepper (cayenne) (add more if you want a noticeable kick!) [2, 3, 4] 
* 

How to use it: Pat your steaks completely dry with a paper towel. If you have oil, drizzle a tiny bit on the meat to help the spices stick; otherwise, press the dry rub generously onto both sides of the meat. Let the steaks sit at room temperature for 20–30 minutes while you prep the grill.
