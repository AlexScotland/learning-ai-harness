## 🌽 The Spiced Corn Butter
Instead of a dry rub, using your spices to make a compound butter will give you that diner-style, melt-in-your-mouth grilled corn. [7, 8] 
Soften 4 tablespoons of butter and mix in:

* 
* 1 tsp Vegetable spices
* 1/2 tsp Paprika
* 1/4 tsp White pepper (adds a sharp, subtle warmth)
* A tiny pinch Nutmeg & Cinnamon (Just a dusting! In very small amounts, these warm spices give grilled corn an incredible, cozy depth without making it taste like dessert).
* A pinch Salt
* 

How to use it: Once your corn is done soaking, shake off the excess water. You can either pull the husks back, brush them with a little plain butter, pull the husks back up, and grill them—or grill them completely bare over the grates until charred. The second you pull the hot corn off the grill, slather them generously with this Spiced Corn Butter so it melts into all the crevices.
